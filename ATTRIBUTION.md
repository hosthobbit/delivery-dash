# Attribution

## Original to this project

Everything below was made for Delivery Dash by Host Hobbit, in code:

- **3D models.** Houses, shops, bike and rider, people, dog, cars, street furniture and trees are built from primitive shapes by `Assets/DeliveryDash/Editor/MeshKit.cs` and `Prefabs.cs`.
- **Textures.** Brick, render, slate, tarmac, paving, grass, leaves, wood, newsprint and signs are drawn procedurally by `Assets/DeliveryDash/Editor/TextureKit.cs`.
- **Sound effects and music.** These are synthesised at runtime by `Assets/DeliveryDash/Scripts/Audio/Sfx.cs`. No recorded audio is used.
- **Shop names and street details.** The shop names (PATEL'S NEWS, THE DAILY GRIND, SUDS LAUNDERETTE) are fictional.
- **Game design, code, UI and the WebGL loader page.**

No third-party art, models, sounds or code snippets were imported.
